FAQ
Is PDF Nest really free?

Yes. Every tool is unlocked from the first launch — there is no premium tier and no in-app purchase. The app shows ads, and that is how it stays free.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in screen at all. Install it, grant file access once, and everything works.

Why does it ask for All files access?

So the Home list can find every PDF already stored on your phone instead of making you pick files one at a time through the system picker. The app only reads and manages the documents it shows you, and never uploads their contents.

Do my documents get uploaded anywhere?

No. Reading, annotation, OCR and translation all run on the device itself. Your files are not sent to a server for processing.

How good is Word, Excel and PowerPoint conversion?

It is best-effort. Plain documents convert cleanly; very complex layouts may shift. We would rather say that up front than promise a perfect match.
