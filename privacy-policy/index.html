Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Applies to PDF Nest - Reader & Editor

PDF Nest - Reader & Editor ("PDF Nest", "the App") is developed and published by Khanh Thanh LTD ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at khanhthanhltd@gmail.com.

1. Summary of what this app does with your files

PDF Nest lets you view, annotate, convert, and manage PDF and office documents that are stored on your device. Reading, annotating (highlight/underline/strikethrough/draw/signature), OCR (Image to Text), on-device translation, and file conversion (Scan, Image↔PDF, Word/Excel/PPT→PDF, Split, Merge, Compress, ZIP) are all performed locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes the contents of your documents, and we do not upload your files to Khanh Thanh LTD or to any third party as part of these features. If you choose to share a file (for example via the system share sheet), that transfer is initiated by you and goes to the destination app or contact you pick — it does not pass through our servers.

PDF Nest is free to use. All features are unlocked and available immediately — the App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or make a purchase to use it.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Files and content you choose to open

The App requests All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) and, on older Android versions, storage read access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) so it can list, open, edit, convert, and manage PDF and office files stored anywhere on your device — this is the core function of a file-management app and is why the permission is broader than a single folder. We do not read, transmit, or analyze the content of your files for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond performing the action you request (viewing, annotating, converting, OCR, translation).

2.2 Device, usage, and advertising information (via third-party SDKs)

The App uses the following third-party software development kits (SDKs) to serve ads, measure install attribution, and understand app usage and stability. These SDKs may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / AD_ID) and similar mobile identifiers
  • Privacy Sandbox on Android signals (Topics, Attribution, and Custom Audience APIs, on devices/OS versions that support them) used by our advertising SDKs as part of Google's platform-level transition away from the Advertising ID
  • Device information (device model, OS version, language, screen size, network type)
  • App usage and interaction events (screens viewed, buttons tapped, session length)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (not precise GPS location — the App does not request location permission)
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data
  • Install referrer / attribution data (which ad or store listing led to the install)

We do not link this information to your name, email, or any account, because PDF Nest does not have user accounts or sign-in.

2.3 Notifications

The App requests notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) to show local notifications about app activity (for example, when a conversion or scan finishes). This does not require sending your data to a server.

4. How we use information

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App's core file-management, viewing, annotation, OCR, translation, and conversion features (all performed on-device)
  • Serve and measure the performance of in-app advertising
  • Understand which install channel or campaign brought you to the App (attribution)
  • Diagnose crashes and stability issues
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use

We do not sell your personal information for money.

5. Data sharing

We share the categories of information in § 2.2 with the third-party providers listed in § 3 for the purposes described above. We do not share the content of your PDF/office files with any third party through these SDKs — that data never leaves your device through the App.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because PDF Nest does not require an account, we do not hold a server-side profile tied to your identity that we can delete on request. Advertising and analytics identifiers are retained by the third-party providers listed above according to their own retention policies. You can:

  • Reset or opt out of your Advertising ID in your device settings (Android Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, or Google Settings ▸ Ads ▸ "Opt out of Ads Personalization")
  • Uninstall the App to remove all files, settings, and cached data stored locally on your device
  • Contact us at khanhthanhltd@gmail.com if you believe we hold information about you and would like it reviewed or removed; we will respond and coordinate with the relevant SDK provider where applicable

7. Children's privacy

PDF Nest is a general-purpose productivity tool intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at khanhthanhltd@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we or our SDK providers hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing (including ad personalization), and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise these rights for data collected by us, contact khanhthanhltd@gmail.com. For data collected directly by a third-party SDK provider, you may also contact that provider or use their opt-out tools referenced in § 3 and § 6.

9. Data security

We rely on industry-standard security practices provided by our third-party SDK providers (e.g., encrypted transport / HTTPS) for any data those SDKs transmit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
